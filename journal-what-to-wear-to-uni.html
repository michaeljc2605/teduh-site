What to wear to uni at 34°C: one white shirt

How to wear The Air Shirt in White for the walk to class, the cold lecture theatre and presentation day.

Start with the walk to campus

Picture a morning that starts with a walk from the MRT and ends in a cold lecture theatre. The Air Shirt in White is 100% cotton poplin with an open weave and an oversized, dropped-shoulder cut, so it sits away from the body on the walk over. Wear it untucked over short black shorts and roll the sleeves while you are outside. Drift Shorts in Black give you the same look with an elastic waist, which is handy when most of your day is spent sitting.

In the photo

Shop the look

Make the same shirt work indoors

Roll the sleeves back down once you sit down in an air-conditioned room. If the lecture theatre is very cold, button the shirt up to the collar. The roomy cut leaves space for a plain tee underneath, so you can add a layer without changing the look. White keeps the outfit easy to repeat even if you change bags or shoes.

Keep the white crisp through the week

A white shirt shows marks quickly on a long campus day. Check the collar and cuffs when you get home and deal with small marks before they set. Wash it with other light colours and follow the care label. Dry it on a hanger so the collar and placket keep their shape for the next class.

What changes on presentation day?

Keep the shirt and change the bottoms. Swap the shorts for The Breeze Pant in Espresso if a low-rise, full-length cut suits you, then button the Air Shirt and try a small front tuck. Sit down in the outfit for a few minutes before you leave. That will tell you more about whether it works for a long day than a mirror check alone.

Save this formula

Class day: white Air Shirt + black shorts, sleeves rolled. Cold lecture theatre: sleeves down, buttoned to the collar. Presentation day: the same shirt + The Breeze Pant.

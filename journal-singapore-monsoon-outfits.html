Surviving monsoon season in style

What to wear when the commute goes from downpour to aircon.

Dress for the exposed part of the route

If you can wear shorts where you are going, Drift Shorts give you a quick-dry nylon option with an above-the-knee hem. Pair them with the Rose Wash Tee for a casual day. Choose shoes with grip for slick pavement and put your umbrella in a pocket you can reach easily. For a workday that requires trousers, check that the hems clear the ground in your commute shoes.

From the catalogue

Catalogue snapshots

Keep a dry layer for the other end

The Suede Zip Jacket has a brushed suede-touch finish and a stand collar. Carry it in a dry compartment for the office, cinema or train. Use an umbrella or dedicated rain layer outdoors, then pull the jacket on once you are sheltered. If the walk was long, a spare tee gives you something dry to wear underneath it.

Build a rainy-day routine you can repeat

  1. Before you go: Check which sections of the route are exposed. Put the umbrella where you can reach it and separate it from your dry clothes.
  2. When it starts raining: Take covered walkways when available and slow down on smooth surfaces.
  3. After you get inside: Hang wet layers where they can dry. Change your top or shoes if you need to spend the afternoon indoors.
What to pack for a full day out

Umbrella, spare tee and a separate place for wet items. Add the Suede Zip Jacket if you know your destination will be cold indoors.

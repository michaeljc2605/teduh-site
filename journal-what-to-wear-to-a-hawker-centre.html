Dressing for a hawker centre lunch

Easy layers for a lunch break that turns into an afternoon out.

Weekend lunch: build around the walk

Start with the Rose Wash Tee and Drift Shorts. The tee is boxy and the shorts have a relaxed fit and elastic waist, so the pairing works when lunch involves a queue, a crowded table and a walk afterwards. The washed black tee and black shorts make a simple base. Add a light bag and the shoes you already know you can walk in.

Lunch between meetings: keep your office base

Try The Air Shirt in Blue Stripe with The Breeze Pant in Espresso. Roll the shirt cuffs before you sit down so they stay away from the tray. The full-length trousers suit a desk day, so check the hems with your shoes if your lunch route includes wet pavement. For a lunch where your workplace allows shorts, the Air Shirt also works open over the Rose Wash Tee with Drift Shorts.

Small details that make the meal easier

Choose a bag you can keep close to you while both hands are on the tray. A small pack of tissues is useful for spills, and a folding umbrella comes in handy if part of the walk is exposed. If you have another appointment right after lunch, a quick check of the cuffs and front hem before leaving gives you time to deal with a splash.

Two ways to repeat it

Weekend: Rose Wash Tee + Drift Shorts. Workday: Blue Stripe Air Shirt + Espresso Breeze Pant.

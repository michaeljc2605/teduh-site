The 5-piece Southeast Asia packing list

Five pieces, three outfit formulas and room left in your carry-on.

The five pieces to build around

Pack for the plans you already have. For a weekend with a flight, a morning of walking and one dinner out, two bottoms and three tops give you enough range to change the outfit without starting over. Start with the neutral bottoms, then choose which striped top you want to wear each day.

From the catalogue

Catalogue snapshots

  1. The Air Shirt in Blue Stripe. Wear the oversized cotton-poplin button-up open over a tee on the flight or buttoned for dinner.
  2. Rose Wash Tee in Washed Black. Use the boxy cotton-jersey tee as your base for the longest travel day.
  3. Stripe Knit Polo in Light Stripe. Save the contrast collar and fine knit for a café stop or meal where you want a slightly dressier option.
  4. The Breeze Pant in Espresso. The low-rise, wide-leg silhouette works with both the button-up and the polo. Check that the full-length hems clear your travel shoes.
  5. Drift Shorts in Black. Choose the relaxed quick-dry nylon pair for casual walks or a day by the coast.

Three outfits from the same bag

Weekend outfit plan
PlanOutfit
FlightRose Wash Tee + Breeze Pant + Air Shirt worn open.
Morning outRose Wash Tee + Drift Shorts. Carry the Air Shirt if you want an indoor layer.
DinnerStripe Knit Polo + Breeze Pant. Swap in the buttoned Air Shirt on a second evening.

Adjust the capsule before you leave

If your itinerary includes a place of worship, check its clothing requirements in advance. Keep one covered option easy to reach in your bag. Do a quick try-on with your walking shoes, especially with the Breeze Pant. The five pieces cover the main outfits. Add sleepwear, underwear and any activity-specific clothes separately.

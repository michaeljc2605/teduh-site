Privacy policy

How TEDUH collects, uses and protects your personal data under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). Last updated: 29 October 2026.

1. Who we are

TEDUH (“we”, “us”) is a Singapore clothing label. This website is a student project; TEDUH is a fictional brand used for coursework.

2. What data we collect

  • Voucher form: your name, email address, shopping preference (women / men / both) and preferred store.
  • Contact form: your name, email address, topic and message.
  • Purchases: handled entirely by Shopee or Lazada. We don't collect your payment details on this site.

3. Why we collect it

  • To send the voucher you asked for.
  • To reply to your messages.
  • To send marketing emails, only if you ticked the separate optional box.

4. Consent

We collect data only after you actively tick a consent checkbox. Consent for marketing is always separate and optional. You can withdraw consent at any time by clicking “unsubscribe” in any email, or by emailing our Data Protection Officer. We will act on your request within 10 working days.

5. How long we keep it

Voucher and marketing data: 12 months after your last interaction with us. Contact messages: 6 months. After that, we delete it.

6. How we protect it

Data is stored with a reputable form provider using encrypted connections. Only our team can access it. We never sell or rent your personal data.

7. Sharing

We don't share your data with third parties, except service providers that help us run the forms and email, who must protect it to the same standard.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or withdraw your consent. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies

This site uses only essential cookies needed for it to work. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies without your permission. Our Shopee and Lazada links include campaign tags (UTM parameters) so we can count visits from this site; they don't identify you personally.

10. Contact our Data Protection Officer

Email privacy@teduh.example. If you're not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) Singapore.

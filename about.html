Our story

Born on a 34°C afternoon.

Our name comes from teduh, the Malay and Indonesian word for serenity: the calm you feel in a cool, shaded spot out of the sun. That's what we want our clothes to feel like.

Why we started

It began with a simple frustration: walking ten minutes to the MRT and arriving soaked, then sitting in an office cold enough to need a jacket.

Most clothes in our wardrobes were designed for four seasons. Singapore has one season, with very strong aircon. So we set out to make everyday basics that work for both.

We sell on Shopee and Lazada because that's where Singapore already shops. This site is where we show what makes each piece different.

What we believe

☀

Comfort first

If it's not comfortable in 34°C, it doesn't make the cut.

❖

Fewer, better

Pieces that mix together, so you need less.

✎

Honest info

Real measurements, real fabric details, no hype.

♡

Local at heart

Designed around life in Singapore: MRT, hawker centres, monsoon rain.

Sizing

2 questions

How do your sizes run?
Our cuts are relaxed. If you're between sizes, size down for a neater fit or stay true to size for more airflow. See the size guide.
Are the shirts really unisex?
Yes. The Air Shirt, Breeze Pant, Rose Wash Tee and Suede Zip Jacket are cut to work on all body types. Check the model notes on each product.
02

Fabric & care

3 questions

Will linen crease?
A little, which is part of its charm. Our linen-blend has a softened finish, so creases relax within an hour of wear.
Is the Breeze Pant see-through?
No. It uses a denser twill weave for coverage, even in light colours.
How do I wash my TEEDUH clothes?
Cold, gentle wash and hang to dry in the shade. Read our care guide.
03

Orders & delivery

3 questions

Why do I buy on Shopee or Lazada?
You keep your usual vouchers, coins and buyer protection, and checkout is familiar. This site helps you choose; the stores handle payment.
How long does delivery take?
Usually 2–5 working days within Singapore, depending on the delivery option you choose. See Shipping & Returns.
Can I return or exchange?
Yes, within 30 days if the item is unworn with tags. Start the return through the store you bought from.

Vouchers & your data

3 questions

How do I get 15% off?
Fill in the form on our Get 15% Off page. Your code arrives by email.
What do you do with my email?
We only use it to send your voucher, and to send offers if you separately agreed. Read our Privacy Policy.
How do I unsubscribe?
Click unsubscribe in any email, or write to privacy@teeduh.example and we'll remove you.
05

About us

1 question

Where does the name TEEDUH come from?
From teduh, the Malay and Indonesian word for serenity: the calm you feel in a cool, shaded spot out of the sun. That's how we want our clothes to feel in Singapore's heat. Read our story.
Delivery & returns

Shipping & returns

We sell through our official Shopee and Lazada stores, so delivery and returns follow the store's trusted process.

Delivery in Singapore

OptionTypical timeCost
Standard delivery3–5 working daysFree over S$40, otherwise shown at checkout
Express delivery1–2 working daysShown at checkout

Exact options, times and fees are set by each store and shown at checkout.

Returns

30 days to change your mind.

Request

Open your order in Shopee or Lazada and choose "Return/Refund" within 30 days.

Pack

Items must be unworn, unwashed and have their tags. Use the original mailer if you can.

Refund

Once we receive it, the store processes your refund to your original payment method.

Exchanges

Need another size? Return the item and place a new order. It's the fastest way to get the right size before it sells out.

Can't return

  • Worn or washed items
  • Items without tags
  • Final-sale pieces marked on the store

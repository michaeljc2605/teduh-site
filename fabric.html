Fabric technology

Why TEEDUH keeps you cool.

No magic, just smart choices: open weaves, quick-dry yarns and colours that forgive a humid day.

Three things that matter in 34°C

1. Airflow
Linen fibres are thicker and less regular than cotton, so the weave has tiny gaps. Air moves through, carrying body heat away instead of trapping it.
2. Moisture
Our jersey pieces use a quick-dry yarn that pulls sweat to the outside of the fabric, where it can evaporate. Less cling, less damp.
3. Colour
Very light colours show damp patches; very dark colours absorb sun. That's why we lean on stripes, gingham and polka dots, plus mid-tones like Bone, Tan and Sorbet: busy prints and soft shades both hide it well.
4. Cut
Relaxed shoulders, wide legs and longer hems leave space between fabric and skin, so air can circulate as you walk.

Sustainability

Made to last, not to landfill.

The most sustainable piece of clothing is the one you keep wearing. Here's how we try to make that easier.

Our four promises

  • Small batches. We make limited runs to avoid unsold stock.
  • Natural-leaning fabrics. Linen needs less water to grow than conventional cotton.
  • Plastic-free packaging. Orders ship in recycled paper mailers.
  • Care that extends life. Every piece comes with a care card to keep it going longer.

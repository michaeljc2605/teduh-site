What to wear in Singapore heat: the Off-Shoulder Tie Top

One top for the 34°C walk from the MRT, the 18°C office and dinner after work.

Fabric and fit for a day in the heat

For a day that moves between a warm commute and a cold office, start with the fabric, fit and layers. A relaxed cut leaves room around the body, while a jacket you can take off lets you adjust the outfit through the day.

The Off-Shoulder Tie Top is made from cotton–modal jersey with elastane for stretch. One shoulder is open, the body sits relaxed rather than tight, and the fine stripe gives plain trousers or a skirt a little more detail. The tie at the neck changes the shape: knot it close for the office, or leave it long and loose for the weekend.

In the photo

Shop the look

The commute: keep the bottom half light

In the photo the top is worn with the Suede Panel Skirt in Tan, an A-line midi in lined microfibre faux suede. It suits the aircon or evening part of the day. On the hottest days, swap the boots for open sandals. For a cotton option, the Wrap Maxi Skirt is 100% cotton twill with a tie waist.

The office: one layer for the aircon

CBD offices often sit around 18–20°C. Keep the Suede Zip Jacket on your chair and zip it over the top when the aircon kicks in. With the jacket on and the tie knotted close, the outfit works for smart-casual desk days. For client meetings or a formal office, check your dress code before you wear an off-shoulder top on its own.

After work: change the tie, not the outfit

Take the jacket off, undo the knot and leave the tie loose down the front. That’s enough of a change for dinner or drinks. For weekend brunch, the Sorbet colour does the same job in a softer pink.

Quick answers

What should women wear to the office in Singapore’s heat? Choose a relaxed cut and check the fabric composition before buying. The Off-Shoulder Tie Top uses 50% cotton, 45% modal and 5% elastane jersey. Pair it with a skirt or wide trousers and keep a jacket at your desk for the aircon, if your dress code allows an off-shoulder neckline.

Is an off-shoulder top OK for a Singapore office? It can suit a casual or smart-casual office, especially with a jacket at your desk. Check your dress code for client meetings or formal workplaces.

Will this top hide sweat? The fine stripe is a design detail, not a guarantee that damp patches will be hidden. Choose the fit and colour you prefer and allow time to cool down after the commute.
